# 🎯 Nutrition Goals: lean bulk

[[Dashboards/Food|← Food]] · [[Home]]

## Where I am
- Started training ~3 weeks ago at **71.5 kg**. Now **68.7 kg** (27 Oct), dropping roughly 0.8–1 kg a week.
- ≥183 cm, 5 training days a week. At this weight and activity, maintenance is about **2700–2800 kcal**.
- Losing ~1 kg a week means I've been eating about **800 kcal a day too little**: the serek bowl, the shake and 2 meals, with the 3rd meal often skipped.

## Daily targets

| | Target | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| **Calories** | **3000 kcal** | Maintenance + ~250–300. Gain slowly, mostly muscle |
| Protein | **170 g** (minimum) | ~2.5 g/kg. The shake + serek already give ~110 g |
| Fat | 90 g | Calorie-dense, easy calories (oil, nuts, peanut butter, cheese) |
| Carbs | 375 g | Fuel for 5 sessions a week. Rice, buckwheat, pasta, bread, potatoes |
| Fiber | 35 g | |

> [!tip] Calories are what matters here
> On these recipes protein ends up around 230–260 g, well above the target. That's fine, but protein is very filling. **When I'm full, add carbs/fat (extra rice, bread, olive oil, nuts), not more meat.**

These targets are set in the Macros plugin, so the progress bars in each daily note use them.

## Weigh-in rule
1. Weigh every morning after the toilet, before food. Log it in the daily note's ⚖️ **Weight** field.
2. Every Sunday, compare this week's **7-day average** with last week's. A single day means nothing (water, salt, carbs).
3. Goal: **+0.25–0.5 kg a week**.
   - Average not up after **2 weeks** → add **+200 kcal** (e.g. a second shake, or 1 extra slice of bread + PB).
   - Up **more than 0.7 kg a week** → take away 150 kcal.
   - In the first week, expect +0.5–1 kg of water and glycogen from eating more carbs. That's normal.

## Cooking plan (2 sessions, ~2.5 h total)
- **Sunday:** Goulash Soup (simmers on its own) + Honey Garlic Chicken + Greek Pasta Salad → covers Mon–Wed.
- **Wednesday:** Creamy Paprika Pork + Tuna Bean Salad → covers Thu–Sat. Freeze 1–2 goulash portions for Sat/Sun.

## Not skipping the 3rd meal
- When I'm hungry and too lazy to reheat → **eat a salad cold**. That's the reason there are two cold salads.
- Emergency options with no cooking: a **second shake**, 2 slices of bread + peanut butter + banana (~500 kcal), or serek + nuts.
- Set a phone reminder ~3 h after lunch. With low appetite, hunger often shows up too late.
- Drink fluids *after* meals rather than before, so they don't fill me up.
